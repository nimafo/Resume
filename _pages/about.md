I am a PhD Candidate at Environmental and Climatic Design section in the Department of AET at TU Delft. My PhD research centers on modelling daylight for existing indoor spaces. During the past years, my research was focused on daylight modelling, dynamic energy simulation, and indoor geometry reconstruction. I am interested in sustainable and evidence-based development of the built environment.
